# Privacy Policy

**App:** Point Ranking (iPhone)
**Last updated:** 11 November 2026

Point Ranking helps you log your daily phone calls, TG visits and non-TG visits and track them against monthly targets. This policy explains what happens to that information.

## The short version

- We don't collect, see, store or sell any of your data.
- There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- Your data stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

## What the app stores

The app stores only what you enter: the number of calls and visits for each day, optional notes, and the public holidays and leave days you mark. It also stores your theme choice.

## Where it is stored

- **On your iPhone**, in the app's private storage.
- **In your iCloud account**, if you're signed in to iCloud. The app uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage so your data syncs between your devices and comes back if you reinstall the app. This data sits in your own iCloud account under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We, the developer, have no access to it.

We don't run any servers, so your data is never sent to us.

## Backup and export files

When you tap **Back Up Now** or **Export for Excel (CSV)**, the app creates a file and saves or shares it **only where you choose**, for example iCloud Drive, Files, Mail or WhatsApp. What happens to the file after that is up to you and the service you send it to.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks or analytics tools.

## Children

The app is a work tool and isn't directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

- Clearing a day in the app removes it on all your devices.
- Deleting the app removes the copy on that iPhone.
- Data synced through iCloud is managed by Apple as part of your iCloud account.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
