# Point Ranking Support

Point Ranking is a daily tracker for phone calls, TG visits and non-TG visits against fixed monthly targets.

**Need help?** Email **abhishekdesai1239@gmail.com**. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were trying to do.

## How the targets work

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly targets | 250 calls, 70 visits, 49 TG visits (70% of visits) |
| Working days | Days in the month − Sundays − public holidays − planned leave |
| Call / visit days | 40% of working days are call days, 60% are visit days |
| Daily numbers | Target ÷ call days (calls) or ÷ visit days (visits, TG), rounded up |

Holidays and leave don't lower the targets. They leave fewer working days, so the daily numbers go up. **Analytics** shows the numbers needed from today, re-spread over the working days left.

## Common questions

**How do I log a day?**
Open **Today**, pick Calls, TG or Non-TG, set the number with − / + (or the +5 / +10 buttons) and tap **Save Activity**. Use the arrows at the top to fill in an earlier day.

**How do I mark a holiday or leave?**
Open **Calendar**, tap the day, and switch on **Public holiday** or **Planned leave**. Sundays are never working days, so they can't be marked.

**Will I lose my data if I delete the app or change phones?**
Not if you're signed in to iCloud. The app syncs your data to your iCloud account automatically, and it comes back when you install the app again with the same Apple ID. You can also keep your own copy: **Config → Back Up Now** saves a backup file, and **Restore from Backup** loads it.

**Can I open my data in Excel?**
Yes: **Config → Export for Excel (CSV)**, then send the file to yourself or save it to Files.

**Can I change the targets?**
No. The targets and the 40/60 split are fixed for everyone using the app.

**Can I change how the app looks?**
Yes: **Config → Appearance** has four colour themes and Light/Dark mode.
